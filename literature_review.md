Literature Review

1\. Smith et al. (1988), "Using the ADAP Learning Algorithm to Forecast the Onset of Diabetes Mellitus"

Researchers used a neural network called ADAP to predict diabetes in high-risk people. It achieved a sensitivity and specificity score of 0.76 and was compared with other prediction methods.

2\. Alzboon et al. (2025), "A Comparative Study of Machine Learning Techniques for Early Prediction of Diabetes"

This study used 8 machine learning algorithms to predict diabetes from data of 768 patients. The Neural Network performed best with 78.57% accuracy, followed by Random Forest with 76.30% accuracy.

3\. Wajahat \& Keshtkar (2024), "Advancing Precision Healthcare Analytics: Machine Learning Approaches for Diabetes Prognosis using the PIMA Indian Dataset"

This study used machine learning to predict diabetes using the PIMA Indian dataset. It found that glucose level is the most important factor, and Logistic Regression performed better than Random Forest, KNN, and Deep Neural Networks.

4\. Paul et al. (2025), "A Comparative Analysis on Diabetes using Ensemble Machine Learning Models on Pima Indian Datasets"

This study used machine learning models to predict diabetes from the Pima Indians dataset. Random Forest and XGBoost performed best, achieving over 88% accuracy and helping detect diabetes early.
